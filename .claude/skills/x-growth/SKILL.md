# X Growth Strategy (SPARK Framework)

Core philosophy: **conversation over broadcasting**. Replies are ~27× more valuable than likes. Author-reply chains yield ~150× impact. First-hour velocity determines out-of-network reach.

## SPARK Framework

- **S**park — native, reply-worthy content. Links go in first reply, NOT in the post itself
- **P**remium & profile — optimize bio (formula: I help [audience] achieve [result]), pinned post = best thread with CTA
- **A**mplify — strategic replies into larger niche accounts (60% of effort)
- **R**ace the first hour — post when your audience is active, then engage immediately
- **K**eep credible — no pods, no bait, no copy-paste virality

## Content Split (weekly)

- 40% value posts (tips, data, how-tos)
- 25% opinion posts (contrarian takes backed by data)
- 25% story posts (personal experience, process documentation)
- 10% engagement posts (questions, polls)

## Reply Strategy

- Target accounts with 2-10× your follower count
- Reply within 15 min of their posts going live
- 2-3 sentences max, add genuine value (data, story, perspective)
- Never: "Great post!" or generic praise

## Critical Boundaries

- Agent drafts posts/replies — humans execute live engagement
- No auto-reply spam, no coordinated amplification
- Analytics from native X tools only — never fabricated
- Negative/combative content gets throttled by algorithm — stay constructive
